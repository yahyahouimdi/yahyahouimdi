### 🛰️ Remote Sensing Image Captioning (RSIC)
**PFA / AI Research Project — ENIT** · *Nov 2025 – Apr 2026*

Research project generating natural-language descriptions from satellite imagery using **SwinV2 and Transformer-based architectures**.

* Reproduced and extended the TSFE research baseline
* Replaced the backbone with **SwinV2-Base** and added a 6-layer Transformer decoder, alongside critical bug fixes
* Achieved a **+136.7% CIDEr improvement** over the reproduced baseline
* Worked with **RSICD, UCM-Captions, and Sydney-Captions** datasets

[→ View project](https://github.com/yahyahouimdi/RSIC_remote_sensing_image_captioning)

That transformer-and-pipeline experience carried directly into my next project — trading satellite imagery for real-time health data, but keeping the same focus on event-driven, decision-making architectures.
